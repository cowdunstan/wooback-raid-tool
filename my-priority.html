Loot

My raid loot priority

Pick a raid and the guild loot sheets turn into a per-boss list of what your own characters can roll on — no signup needed. Up top, “What you can roll on” pulls out where you hold named prio, grouped by tier and naming the classes ahead of you. Below, every boss lists all its items, with the ones you can roll marked: open MS > OS for anything, or your prio tier where the sheet names your spec. Black Temple and Hyjal come from the P3 sheet, SSC and Tempest Keep from the P2 one. Officers can point the whole page at any raider with the “View as” picker.

Prio comes from the guild loot sheets — edits happen there, not here.
